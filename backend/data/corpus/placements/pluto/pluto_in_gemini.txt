Pluto in Gemini — Intensity, power, control, and the deep changes that reshape how you relate to yourself
There’s something curious, flexible, and mentally quick about the way this part of you works. Words and information can carry real power for you; once your mind locks onto something, you want the underlying truth. You rarely experience this part of life in a completely casual way; it tends to get under your skin. At your best, this placement supports asking the question that opens a stuck situation. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. The growth edge is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. Real strength comes from letting transformation happen without turning vulnerability into a battle.
