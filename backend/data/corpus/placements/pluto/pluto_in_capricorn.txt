Pluto in Capricorn — Intensity, power, control, and the deep changes that reshape how you relate to yourself
There’s something contained, strategic, and quietly ambitious about the way this part of you works. Power, authority, ambition, and control can become major themes, especially around who gets to define success. You rarely experience this part of life in a completely casual way; it tends to get under your skin. At your best, this placement supports turning long-term intention into something concrete. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward carrying too much alone, measuring yourself by output, or becoming rigid under pressure. The growth edge is letting competence include rest, support, and emotional honesty. Real strength comes from letting transformation happen without turning vulnerability into a battle.
