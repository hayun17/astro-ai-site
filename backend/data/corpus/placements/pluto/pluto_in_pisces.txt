Pluto in Pisces — Intensity, power, control, and the deep changes that reshape how you relate to yourself
There’s something sensitive, imaginative, and porous about the way this part of you works. Emotional and spiritual boundaries can be transformative territory, especially when empathy starts blending into self-erasure. You rarely experience this part of life in a completely casual way; it tends to get under your skin. At your best, this placement supports feeling nuance, symbolism, and emotional undercurrents that are easy to miss.
