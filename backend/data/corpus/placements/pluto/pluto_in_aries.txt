Pluto in Aries — Intensity, power, control, and the deep changes that reshape how you relate to yourself
There’s something direct, brave, and quick to move about the way this part of you works. Power is tied to autonomy and action, so being blocked or dominated can trigger an unusually strong response. You rarely experience this part of life in a completely casual way; it tends to get under your skin. At your best, this placement supports trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance.
