Pluto in Sagittarius — Intensity, power, control, and the deep changes that reshape how you relate to yourself
There’s something open, candid, and possibility-seeking about the way this part of you works. Beliefs can be transformative territory, and changing your mind may sometimes feel like changing your whole identity. You rarely experience this part of life in a completely casual way; it tends to get under your skin. At your best, this placement supports finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. The growth edge is keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters. Real strength comes from letting transformation happen without turning vulnerability into a battle.
