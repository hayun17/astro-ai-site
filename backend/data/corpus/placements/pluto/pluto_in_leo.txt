Pluto in Leo — Intensity, power, control, and the deep changes that reshape how you relate to yourself
There’s something warm, expressive, and heart-led about the way this part of you works. Being visible can feel powerful and vulnerable at the same time, especially when pride is protecting something tender. You rarely experience this part of life in a completely casual way; it tends to get under your skin. At your best, this placement supports bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. The growth edge is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. Real strength comes from letting transformation happen without turning vulnerability into a battle.
