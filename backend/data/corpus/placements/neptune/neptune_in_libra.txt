Neptune in Libra — Imagination, longing, intuition, and the places where your boundaries can become soft
There’s something relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware about the way this part of you works. You can see the best in people and relationships so vividly that reality sometimes takes longer to register. You can be unusually receptive here, which makes this area inspiring but sometimes hard to read clearly. At your best, this placement supports seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said. The growth edge is choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate. Your gift becomes strongest when imagination and reality are allowed to work together.
