Neptune in Leo — Imagination, longing, intuition, and the places where your boundaries can become soft
There’s something warm, expressive, and heart-led about the way this part of you works. Creative fantasy is strong, and you may romanticize being seen, adored, or fully understood through self-expression. You can be unusually receptive here, which makes this area inspiring but sometimes hard to read clearly. At your best, this placement supports bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. The growth edge is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. Your gift becomes strongest when imagination and reality are allowed to work together.
