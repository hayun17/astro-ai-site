Neptune in Sagittarius — Imagination, longing, intuition, and the places where your boundaries can become soft
There’s something open, candid, and possibility-seeking about the way this part of you works. Meaning, faith, travel, and big ideas can become places of inspiration — and occasionally places where you over-believe. You can be unusually receptive here, which makes this area inspiring but sometimes hard to read clearly. At your best, this placement supports finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. The growth edge is keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters. Your gift becomes strongest when imagination and reality are allowed to work together.
