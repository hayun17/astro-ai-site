Neptune in Gemini — Imagination, longing, intuition, and the places where your boundaries can become soft
There’s something curious, flexible, and mentally quick about the way this part of you works. Your imagination moves through words, stories, symbols, and associations, though facts can blur when you are emotionally invested. You can be unusually receptive here, which makes this area inspiring but sometimes hard to read clearly. At your best, this placement supports asking the question that opens a stuck situation. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. The growth edge is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. Your gift becomes strongest when imagination and reality are allowed to work together.
