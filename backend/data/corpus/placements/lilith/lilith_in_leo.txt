Lilith in Leo — Instinct, unapologetic truth, boundaries, and the part of you that dislikes being edited into acceptability
There’s something warm, expressive, and heart-led about the way this part of you works. There is a fierce part of you that refuses to be made small, invisible, or embarrassed for wanting recognition and creative freedom. There can be a strong “don’t tell me who I’m allowed to be” feeling around this part of you. At your best, this placement supports bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. The growth edge is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. Integrated well, it becomes a clean sense of self-respect and a more honest relationship with desire and anger.
