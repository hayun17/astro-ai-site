Lilith in Pisces — Instinct, unapologetic truth, boundaries, and the part of you that dislikes being edited into acceptability
There’s something sensitive, imaginative, and porous about the way this part of you works. Your raw edge appears where compassion turns into self-erasure; a major boundary is learning that empathy does not mean unlimited access to you. There can be a strong “don’t tell me who I’m allowed to be” feeling around this part of you. At your best, this placement supports feeling nuance, symbolism, and emotional undercurrents that are easy to miss. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward blurring boundaries, idealizing, or disappearing into fantasy when reality feels too sharp. The growth edge is keeping your softness while giving it structure and clear edges. Integrated well, it becomes a clean sense of self-respect and a more honest relationship with desire and anger.
