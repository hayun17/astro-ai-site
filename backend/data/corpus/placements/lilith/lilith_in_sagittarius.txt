Lilith in Sagittarius — Instinct, unapologetic truth, boundaries, and the part of you that dislikes being edited into acceptability
There’s something open, candid, and possibility-seeking about the way this part of you works. Your raw truth needs freedom to question beliefs, speak plainly, and reject moral rules that feel hypocritical or confining. There can be a strong “don’t tell me who I’m allowed to be” feeling around this part of you. At your best, this placement supports finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. The growth edge is keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters. Integrated well, it becomes a clean sense of self-respect and a more honest relationship with desire and anger.
