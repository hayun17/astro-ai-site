Lilith in Gemini — Instinct, unapologetic truth, boundaries, and the part of you that dislikes being edited into acceptability
There’s something curious, flexible, and mentally quick about the way this part of you works. Your unfiltered voice can be sharp, funny, provocative, or impossible to keep inside once something feels intellectually dishonest. There can be a strong “don’t tell me who I’m allowed to be” feeling around this part of you. At your best, this placement supports asking the question that opens a stuck situation. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. The growth edge is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. Integrated well, it becomes a clean sense of self-respect and a more honest relationship with desire and anger.
