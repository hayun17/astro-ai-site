Jupiter in Leo — Growth, confidence, meaning, and the way you make life feel bigger
There’s something warm, expressive, and heart-led about the way this part of you works. Confidence expands when you create, perform, lead, or simply let yourself be visibly enthusiastic. You tend to expand when you let this part of you take up a little more room. At your best, this placement supports bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. The growth edge is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. The sweet spot is confidence with perspective, not confidence that has to prove itself.
