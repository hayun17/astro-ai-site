Uranus in Sagittarius — Independence, disruption, originality, and the part of you that refuses stale rules
There’s something open, candid, and possibility-seeking about the way this part of you works. Your beliefs need room to evolve; rigid worldviews can make you restless very quickly. You need enough freedom here to experiment instead of simply repeating what already exists. At your best, this placement supports finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. The growth edge is keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters. Your edge is learning how to reinvent without blowing up what still has value.
