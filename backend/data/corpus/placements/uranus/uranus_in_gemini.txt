Uranus in Gemini — Independence, disruption, originality, and the part of you that refuses stale rules
There’s something curious, flexible, and mentally quick about the way this part of you works. Your mind likes unusual connections, fast shifts, and ideas that break the expected sequence. You need enough freedom here to experiment instead of simply repeating what already exists. At your best, this placement supports asking the question that opens a stuck situation. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. The growth edge is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. Your edge is learning how to reinvent without blowing up what still has value.
