Uranus in Virgo — Independence, disruption, originality, and the part of you that refuses stale rules
There’s something observant, practical, and improvement-minded about the way this part of you works. You are wired to improve systems differently, often spotting a strange-but-effective solution that other people miss. You need enough freedom here to experiment instead of simply repeating what already exists. At your best, this placement supports seeing the small adjustment that makes everything work better. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward overthinking, self-editing, or trying to earn peace by getting everything right. The growth edge is allowing “good and real” to count, even when it is not perfect. Your edge is learning how to reinvent without blowing up what still has value.
