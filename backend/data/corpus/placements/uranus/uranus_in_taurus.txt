Uranus in Taurus — Independence, disruption, originality, and the part of you that refuses stale rules
There’s something steady, sensory, and grounded about the way this part of you works. You can be unexpectedly radical about security, money, values, or the way a stable life should actually look. You need enough freedom here to experiment instead of simply repeating what already exists. At your best, this placement supports building something slowly enough that it can actually last. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. The growth edge is remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness. Your edge is learning how to reinvent without blowing up what still has value.
