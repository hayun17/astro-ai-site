Uranus in Pisces — Independence, disruption, originality, and the part of you that refuses stale rules
There’s something sensitive, imaginative, and porous about the way this part of you works. Your intuition can work in flashes, dreams, images, or odd connections that arrive before you can explain them logically. You need enough freedom here to experiment instead of simply repeating what already exists. At your best, this placement supports feeling nuance, symbolism, and emotional undercurrents that are easy to miss. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward blurring boundaries, idealizing, or disappearing into fantasy when reality feels too sharp. The growth edge is keeping your softness while giving it structure and clear edges. Your edge is learning how to reinvent without blowing up what still has value.
