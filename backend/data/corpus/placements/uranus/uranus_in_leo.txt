Uranus in Leo — Independence, disruption, originality, and the part of you that refuses stale rules
There’s something warm, expressive, and heart-led about the way this part of you works. Creative self-expression works best when it feels unmistakably yours, not when it is optimized to please everyone. You need enough freedom here to experiment instead of simply repeating what already exists. At your best, this placement supports bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. The growth edge is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. Your edge is learning how to reinvent without blowing up what still has value.
