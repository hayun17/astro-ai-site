True Node in Sagittarius — Your growth direction — the qualities that can feel unfamiliar at first but become more natural with practice
There’s something open, candid, and possibility-seeking about the way this part of you works. Growth asks you to build a bigger perspective, take meaningful risks, and stand behind beliefs you have actually examined. This is less about a fixed destiny and more about a direction that stretches you beyond old reflexes. At your best, this placement supports finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. The growth edge is keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters. Growth here works best as a gradual choice, not a personality you have to force yourself to become.
