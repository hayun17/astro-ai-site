True Node in Leo — Your growth direction — the qualities that can feel unfamiliar at first but become more natural with practice
There’s something warm, expressive, and heart-led about the way this part of you works. Growth asks you to create, take up space, risk being seen, and put more heart into choices that matter. This is less about a fixed destiny and more about a direction that stretches you beyond old reflexes. At your best, this placement supports bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. The growth edge is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. Growth here works best as a gradual choice, not a personality you have to force yourself to become.
