True Node in Taurus — Your growth direction — the qualities that can feel unfamiliar at first but become more natural with practice
There’s something steady, sensory, and grounded about the way this part of you works. Growth asks for simplicity, steadiness, self-worth, and a life that does not need constant emotional intensity to feel meaningful. This is less about a fixed destiny and more about a direction that stretches you beyond old reflexes. At your best, this placement supports building something slowly enough that it can actually last. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. The growth edge is remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness. Growth here works best as a gradual choice, not a personality you have to force yourself to become.
