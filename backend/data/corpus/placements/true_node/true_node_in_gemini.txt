True Node in Gemini — Your growth direction — the qualities that can feel unfamiliar at first but become more natural with practice
There’s something curious, flexible, and mentally quick about the way this part of you works. Growth comes through questions, dialogue, flexibility, and allowing new information to change the story. This is less about a fixed destiny and more about a direction that stretches you beyond old reflexes. At your best, this placement supports asking the question that opens a stuck situation. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. The growth edge is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. Growth here works best as a gradual choice, not a personality you have to force yourself to become.
