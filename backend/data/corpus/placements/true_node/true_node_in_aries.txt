True Node in Aries — Your growth direction — the qualities that can feel unfamiliar at first but become more natural with practice
There’s something direct, brave, and quick to move about the way this part of you works. Growth asks you to practice choosing, initiating, and trusting yourself before every decision is socially approved. This is less about a fixed destiny and more about a direction that stretches you beyond old reflexes. At your best, this placement supports trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. When you’re stretched, the pattern can tip toward rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance. The growth edge is letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately. Growth here works best as a gradual choice, not a personality you have to force yourself to become.
