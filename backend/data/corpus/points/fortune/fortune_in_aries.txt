Part of Fortune in Aries — The style of ease, flow, and natural satisfaction you may access when you are not overcomplicating things
Things often feel more fluid when you allow yourself to be direct, brave, and quick to move instead of forcing a completely different pace. It does not guarantee luck; it points more toward a rhythm that can feel surprisingly natural when you let yourself use it. Your natural advantage here is trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. Under stress, you can slip into rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance. It gets easier when you practice letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
