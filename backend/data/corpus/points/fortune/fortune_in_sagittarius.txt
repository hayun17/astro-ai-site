Part of Fortune in Sagittarius — The style of ease, flow, and natural satisfaction you may access when you are not overcomplicating things
Things often feel more fluid when you allow yourself to be open, candid, and possibility-seeking instead of forcing a completely different pace. It does not guarantee luck; it points more toward a rhythm that can feel surprisingly natural when you let yourself use it. Your natural advantage here is finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. Under stress, you can slip into moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. It gets easier when you practice keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
