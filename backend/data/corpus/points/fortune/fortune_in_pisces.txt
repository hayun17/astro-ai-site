Part of Fortune in Pisces — The style of ease, flow, and natural satisfaction you may access when you are not overcomplicating things
Things often feel more fluid when you allow yourself to be sensitive, imaginative, and porous instead of forcing a completely different pace. It does not guarantee luck; it points more toward a rhythm that can feel surprisingly natural when you let yourself use it. Your natural advantage here is feeling nuance, symbolism, and emotional undercurrents that are easy to miss. Under stress, you can slip into blurring boundaries, idealizing, or disappearing into fantasy when reality feels too sharp. It gets easier when you practice keeping your softness while giving it structure and clear edges — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
