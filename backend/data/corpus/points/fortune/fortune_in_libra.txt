Part of Fortune in Libra — The style of ease, flow, and natural satisfaction you may access when you are not overcomplicating things
Things often feel more fluid when you allow yourself to be relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware instead of forcing a completely different pace. It does not guarantee luck; it points more toward a rhythm that can feel surprisingly natural when you let yourself use it. Your natural advantage here is seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. Under stress, you can slip into delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said. It gets easier when you practice choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
