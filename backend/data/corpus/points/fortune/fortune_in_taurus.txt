Part of Fortune in Taurus — The style of ease, flow, and natural satisfaction you may access when you are not overcomplicating things
Things often feel more fluid when you allow yourself to be steady, sensory, and grounded instead of forcing a completely different pace. It does not guarantee luck; it points more toward a rhythm that can feel surprisingly natural when you let yourself use it. Your natural advantage here is building something slowly enough that it can actually last. Under stress, you can slip into holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. It gets easier when you practice remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
