Part of Fortune in Leo — The style of ease, flow, and natural satisfaction you may access when you are not overcomplicating things
Things often feel more fluid when you allow yourself to be warm, expressive, and heart-led instead of forcing a completely different pace. It does not guarantee luck; it points more toward a rhythm that can feel surprisingly natural when you let yourself use it. Your natural advantage here is bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. Under stress, you can slip into feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. It gets easier when you practice letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
