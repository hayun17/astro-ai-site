Descendant in Gemini — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a curious, flexible, and mentally quick quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is asking the question that opens a stuck situation. Under stress, you can slip into scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. It gets easier when you practice giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
