Descendant in Taurus — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a steady, sensory, and grounded quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is building something slowly enough that it can actually last. Under stress, you can slip into holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. It gets easier when you practice remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
