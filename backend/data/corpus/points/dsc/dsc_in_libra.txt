Descendant in Libra — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. Under stress, you can slip into delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said. It gets easier when you practice choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
