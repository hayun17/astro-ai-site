Descendant in Cancer — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a protective, intuitive, and emotionally responsive quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is noticing what people need before they have the words for it. Under stress, you can slip into retreating into a shell, taking things personally, or overprotecting what feels vulnerable. It gets easier when you practice caring deeply without making yourself responsible for everyone’s emotional weather — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
