Descendant in Leo — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a warm, expressive, and heart-led quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. Under stress, you can slip into feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. It gets easier when you practice letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
