Descendant in Capricorn — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a contained, strategic, and quietly ambitious quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is turning long-term intention into something concrete. Under stress, you can slip into carrying too much alone, measuring yourself by output, or becoming rigid under pressure. It gets easier when you practice letting competence include rest, support, and emotional honesty — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
