Descendant in Aries — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a direct, brave, and quick to move quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. Under stress, you can slip into rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance. It gets easier when you practice letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
