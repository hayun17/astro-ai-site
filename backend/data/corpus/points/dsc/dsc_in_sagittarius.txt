Descendant in Sagittarius — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a open, candid, and possibility-seeking quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. Under stress, you can slip into moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. It gets easier when you practice keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
