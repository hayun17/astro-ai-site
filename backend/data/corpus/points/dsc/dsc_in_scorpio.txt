Descendant in Scorpio — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a intense, private, and emotionally perceptive quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is staying present with truths that other people would rather avoid. Under stress, you can slip into testing loyalty, gripping for control, or protecting tenderness with suspicion. It gets easier when you practice letting depth include trust, not only vigilance — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
