Descendant in Virgo — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a observant, practical, and improvement-minded quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is seeing the small adjustment that makes everything work better. Under stress, you can slip into overthinking, self-editing, or trying to earn peace by getting everything right. It gets easier when you practice allowing “good and real” to count, even when it is not perfect — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
