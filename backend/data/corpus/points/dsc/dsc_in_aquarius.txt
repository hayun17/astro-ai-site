Descendant in Aquarius — The qualities you notice strongly in close partnership and the relational mirror that can challenge or complement you
In close relationships, you tend to notice and respond strongly to people who bring a independent, original, and future-minded quality. You may be drawn to these qualities in others, or discover that relationships keep asking you to develop them in yourself. Your natural advantage here is seeing a different route when everyone else assumes there is only one. Under stress, you can slip into detaching, intellectualizing, or protecting freedom by keeping people at a distance. It gets easier when you practice staying emotionally present without feeling that closeness costs your individuality — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
