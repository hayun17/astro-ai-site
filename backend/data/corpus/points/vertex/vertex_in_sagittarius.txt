Vertex in Sagittarius — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more open, candid, and possibility-seeking response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. Under stress, you can slip into moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. It gets easier when you practice keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
