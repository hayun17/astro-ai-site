Vertex in Taurus — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more steady, sensory, and grounded response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is building something slowly enough that it can actually last. Under stress, you can slip into holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. It gets easier when you practice remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
