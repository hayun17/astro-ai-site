Vertex in Gemini — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more curious, flexible, and mentally quick response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is asking the question that opens a stuck situation. Under stress, you can slip into scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. It gets easier when you practice giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
