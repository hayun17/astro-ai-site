Vertex in Cancer — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more protective, intuitive, and emotionally responsive response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is noticing what people need before they have the words for it. Under stress, you can slip into retreating into a shell, taking things personally, or overprotecting what feels vulnerable. It gets easier when you practice caring deeply without making yourself responsible for everyone’s emotional weather — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
