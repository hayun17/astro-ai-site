Vertex in Capricorn — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more contained, strategic, and quietly ambitious response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is turning long-term intention into something concrete. Under stress, you can slip into carrying too much alone, measuring yourself by output, or becoming rigid under pressure. It gets easier when you practice letting competence include rest, support, and emotional honesty — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
