Vertex in Aries — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more direct, brave, and quick to move response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. Under stress, you can slip into rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance. It gets easier when you practice letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
