Vertex in Libra — The kind of energy that can feel especially noticeable around unexpected encounters or turning-point moments
Unexpected moments can stand out when they pull you toward a more relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware response than you planned. It is best read as a symbolic sensitivity, not a promise that a particular meeting is “fated.” Your natural advantage here is seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. Under stress, you can slip into delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said. It gets easier when you practice choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
