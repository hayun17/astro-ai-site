IC in Leo — Your private base, emotional roots, and what “home” needs to feel like on the inside
In private, your nervous system settles more easily when home allows for a warm, expressive, and heart-led rhythm. This part of the chart is less about image and more about where you go when you do not have to perform for anyone. Your natural advantage here is bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. Under stress, you can slip into feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. It gets easier when you practice letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
