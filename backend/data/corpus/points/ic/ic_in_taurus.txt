IC in Taurus — Your private base, emotional roots, and what “home” needs to feel like on the inside
In private, your nervous system settles more easily when home allows for a steady, sensory, and grounded rhythm. This part of the chart is less about image and more about where you go when you do not have to perform for anyone. Your natural advantage here is building something slowly enough that it can actually last. Under stress, you can slip into holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. It gets easier when you practice remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
