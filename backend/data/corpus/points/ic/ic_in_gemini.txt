IC in Gemini — Your private base, emotional roots, and what “home” needs to feel like on the inside
In private, your nervous system settles more easily when home allows for a curious, flexible, and mentally quick rhythm. This part of the chart is less about image and more about where you go when you do not have to perform for anyone. Your natural advantage here is asking the question that opens a stuck situation. Under stress, you can slip into scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. It gets easier when you practice giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
