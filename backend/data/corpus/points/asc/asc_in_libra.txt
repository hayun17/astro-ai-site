Ascendant in Libra — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware, so you may look more socially aware on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. Under stress, you can slip into delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said. It gets easier when you practice choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
