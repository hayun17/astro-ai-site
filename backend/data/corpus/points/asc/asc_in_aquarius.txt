Ascendant in Aquarius — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be independent, original, and future-minded, so you may look more independent on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is seeing a different route when everyone else assumes there is only one. Under stress, you can slip into detaching, intellectualizing, or protecting freedom by keeping people at a distance. It gets easier when you practice staying emotionally present without feeling that closeness costs your individuality — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
