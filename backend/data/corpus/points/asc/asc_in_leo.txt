Ascendant in Leo — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be warm, expressive, and heart-led, so you may look more confident or expressive on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. Under stress, you can slip into feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. It gets easier when you practice letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
