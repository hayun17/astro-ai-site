Ascendant in Gemini — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be curious, flexible, and mentally quick, so you may look more quick-minded on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is asking the question that opens a stuck situation. Under stress, you can slip into scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. It gets easier when you practice giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
