Ascendant in Taurus — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be steady, sensory, and grounded, so you may look more grounded on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is building something slowly enough that it can actually last. Under stress, you can slip into holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. It gets easier when you practice remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
