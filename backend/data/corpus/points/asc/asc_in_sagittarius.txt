Ascendant in Sagittarius — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be open, candid, and possibility-seeking, so you may look more open and candid on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. Under stress, you can slip into moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. It gets easier when you practice keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
