Ascendant in Aries — The way you enter a room, meet new situations, and instinctively present yourself
Your first move tends to be direct, brave, and quick to move, so you may look more decisive on the outside than you always feel inside. People may first read you through this tone, even though it is only one layer of you. Your natural advantage here is trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. Under stress, you can slip into rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance. It gets easier when you practice letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
