Midheaven in Libra — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. Under stress, you can slip into delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said. It gets easier when you practice choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
