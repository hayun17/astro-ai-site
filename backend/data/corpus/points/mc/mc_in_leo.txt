Midheaven in Leo — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being warm, expressive, and heart-led. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. Under stress, you can slip into feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride. It gets easier when you practice letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
