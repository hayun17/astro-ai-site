Midheaven in Gemini — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being curious, flexible, and mentally quick. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is asking the question that opens a stuck situation. Under stress, you can slip into scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it. It gets easier when you practice giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
