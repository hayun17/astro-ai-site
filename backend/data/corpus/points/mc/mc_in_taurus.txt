Midheaven in Taurus — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being steady, sensory, and grounded. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is building something slowly enough that it can actually last. Under stress, you can slip into holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive. It gets easier when you practice remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
