Midheaven in Capricorn — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being contained, strategic, and quietly ambitious. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is turning long-term intention into something concrete. Under stress, you can slip into carrying too much alone, measuring yourself by output, or becoming rigid under pressure. It gets easier when you practice letting competence include rest, support, and emotional honesty — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
