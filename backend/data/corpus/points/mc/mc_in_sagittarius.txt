Midheaven in Sagittarius — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being open, candid, and possibility-seeking. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. Under stress, you can slip into moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details. It gets easier when you practice keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
