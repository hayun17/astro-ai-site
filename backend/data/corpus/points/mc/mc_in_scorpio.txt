Midheaven in Scorpio — Your public direction, visible strengths, and the style you may grow into around work or reputation
In public or professional settings, you often gain traction by being intense, private, and emotionally perceptive. This is not a fixed career prediction; it is more like the energy you tend to develop when you are being seen for what you contribute. Your natural advantage here is staying present with truths that other people would rather avoid. Under stress, you can slip into testing loyalty, gripping for control, or protecting tenderness with suspicion. It gets easier when you practice letting depth include trust, not only vigilance — you do not have to perform the sign perfectly for it to be useful.
