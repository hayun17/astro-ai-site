11th House in Leo — Friendships, communities, networks, future plans, and the causes or ideas that connect you to something larger
In friendships, community, and future plans, your style tends to be warm, expressive, and heart-led. This can be a real strength when it helps you with bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. The tricky version is feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Leo part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
