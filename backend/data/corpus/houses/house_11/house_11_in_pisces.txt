11th House in Pisces — Friendships, communities, networks, future plans, and the causes or ideas that connect you to something larger
In friendships, community, and future plans, your style tends to be sensitive, imaginative, and porous. This can be a real strength when it helps you with feeling nuance, symbolism, and emotional undercurrents that are easy to miss. The tricky version is blurring boundaries, idealizing, or disappearing into fantasy when reality feels too sharp, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is keeping your softness while giving it structure and clear edges. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Pisces part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
