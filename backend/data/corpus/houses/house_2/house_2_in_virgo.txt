2nd House in Virgo — Money, self-worth, possessions, and the kind of security that helps you relax
Around money, values, and self-worth, your style tends to be observant, practical, and improvement-minded. This can be a real strength when it helps you with seeing the small adjustment that makes everything work better. The tricky version is overthinking, self-editing, or trying to earn peace by getting everything right, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is allowing “good and real” to count, even when it is not perfect. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Virgo part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
