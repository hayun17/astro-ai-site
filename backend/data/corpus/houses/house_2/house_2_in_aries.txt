2nd House in Aries — Money, self-worth, possessions, and the kind of security that helps you relax
Around money, values, and self-worth, your style tends to be direct, brave, and quick to move. This can be a real strength when it helps you with trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. The tricky version is rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Aries part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
