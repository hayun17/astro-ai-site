7th House in Libra — Partnership, commitment, and the qualities that become especially visible in one-to-one relationships
In close partnership, your style tends to be relational, balanced, and aesthetically aware. This can be a real strength when it helps you with seeing more than one side without losing your sense of fairness. The tricky version is delaying a choice, over-accommodating, or smoothing things over before the truth is said, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is choosing honestly even when harmony is not immediate. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Libra part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
