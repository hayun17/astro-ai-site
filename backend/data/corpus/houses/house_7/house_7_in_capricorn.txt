7th House in Capricorn — Partnership, commitment, and the qualities that become especially visible in one-to-one relationships
In close partnership, your style tends to be contained, strategic, and quietly ambitious. This can be a real strength when it helps you with turning long-term intention into something concrete. The tricky version is carrying too much alone, measuring yourself by output, or becoming rigid under pressure, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is letting competence include rest, support, and emotional honesty. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Capricorn part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
