7th House in Scorpio — Partnership, commitment, and the qualities that become especially visible in one-to-one relationships
In close partnership, your style tends to be intense, private, and emotionally perceptive. This can be a real strength when it helps you with staying present with truths that other people would rather avoid. The tricky version is testing loyalty, gripping for control, or protecting tenderness with suspicion, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is letting depth include trust, not only vigilance. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Scorpio part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
