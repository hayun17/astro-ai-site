With Leo on the 1st House, you naturally command attention. There’s warmth, charisma, and expressive energy in how you present yourself. People often perceive confidence — sometimes before you fully feel it. Your identity strengthens when you allow yourself to create, lead, and shine authentically. The shadow is performing for approval, but your real power lies in heartfelt self-expression. You’re here to radiate individuality without apology.
