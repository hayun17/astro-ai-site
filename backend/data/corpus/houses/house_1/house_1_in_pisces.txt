With Pisces on the 1st House, your presence feels gentle, intuitive, and emotionally fluid. Others may experience you as compassionate or dreamy. Your identity is sensitive to environment and energy, which makes you adaptable but sometimes unclear about boundaries. The challenge is losing yourself in others, but your strength lies in empathy and imagination. You’re here to bring softness and spiritual awareness into the world.
