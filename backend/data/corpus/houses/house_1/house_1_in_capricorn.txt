With Capricorn on the 1st House, you present yourself as serious, composed, and responsible. Others often perceive maturity and ambition in you from an early age. Your identity grows through achievement and structure. You may carry strong self-discipline and long-term focus. The challenge is self-pressure, but your gift is endurance and authority. You’re here to build something lasting and earn your success step by step.
