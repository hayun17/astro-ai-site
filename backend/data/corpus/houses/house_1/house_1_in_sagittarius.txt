With Sagittarius on the 1st House, you project optimism, openness, and adventurous spirit. People see enthusiasm and forward movement in you. Your identity expands through learning, travel, and seeking truth. You dislike feeling restricted or confined. The shadow can be restlessness, but your strength lies in vision and inspiration. You’re here to live boldly and encourage others to think bigger.
