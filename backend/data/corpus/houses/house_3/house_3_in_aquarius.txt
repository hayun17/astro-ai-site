3rd House in Aquarius — Communication, learning, siblings/peers, and your everyday mental rhythm
In your everyday thinking and communication, your style tends to be independent, original, and future-minded. This can be a real strength when it helps you with seeing a different route when everyone else assumes there is only one. The tricky version is detaching, intellectualizing, or protecting freedom by keeping people at a distance, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is staying emotionally present without feeling that closeness costs your individuality. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Aquarius part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
