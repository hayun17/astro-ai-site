3rd House in Gemini — Communication, learning, siblings/peers, and your everyday mental rhythm
In your everyday thinking and communication, your style tends to be curious, flexible, and mentally quick. This can be a real strength when it helps you with asking the question that opens a stuck situation. The tricky version is scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Gemini part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
