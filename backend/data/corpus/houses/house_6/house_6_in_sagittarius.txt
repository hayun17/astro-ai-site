6th House in Sagittarius — Daily routines, work habits, health maintenance, and practical responsibility
In routines, work habits, and maintenance, your style tends to be open, candid, and possibility-seeking. This can be a real strength when it helps you with finding meaning, perspective, and a larger horizon when things feel small. The tricky version is moving on too quickly, overpromising, or using optimism to skip uncomfortable details, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is keeping your freedom while staying long enough to deepen what matters. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Sagittarius part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
