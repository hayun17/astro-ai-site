6th House in Taurus — Daily routines, work habits, health maintenance, and practical responsibility
In routines, work habits, and maintenance, your style tends to be steady, sensory, and grounded. This can be a real strength when it helps you with building something slowly enough that it can actually last. The tricky version is holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Taurus part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
