6th House in Aries — Daily routines, work habits, health maintenance, and practical responsibility
In routines, work habits, and maintenance, your style tends to be direct, brave, and quick to move. This can be a real strength when it helps you with trusting your first spark and giving things a real start. The tricky version is rushing, getting impatient, or treating every delay like resistance, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is letting courage stay strong without needing everything to happen immediately. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Aries part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
