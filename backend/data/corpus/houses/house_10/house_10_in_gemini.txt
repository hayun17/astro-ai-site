10th House in Gemini — Career, public life, reputation, and the kind of responsibility you are willing to be known for
In public life and long-term direction, your style tends to be curious, flexible, and mentally quick. This can be a real strength when it helps you with asking the question that opens a stuck situation. The tricky version is scattering your attention or talking around a feeling instead of sitting with it, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is giving your curiosity enough focus to become real understanding. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Gemini part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
