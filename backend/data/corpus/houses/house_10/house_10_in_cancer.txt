10th House in Cancer — Career, public life, reputation, and the kind of responsibility you are willing to be known for
In public life and long-term direction, your style tends to be protective, intuitive, and emotionally responsive. This can be a real strength when it helps you with noticing what people need before they have the words for it. The tricky version is retreating into a shell, taking things personally, or overprotecting what feels vulnerable, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is caring deeply without making yourself responsible for everyone’s emotional weather. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Cancer part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
