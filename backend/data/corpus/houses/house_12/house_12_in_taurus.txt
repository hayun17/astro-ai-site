12th House in Taurus — Rest, solitude, the unconscious, endings, hidden stress, and the inner life that needs space away from noise
In solitude, rest, and your hidden emotional processing, your style tends to be steady, sensory, and grounded. This can be a real strength when it helps you with building something slowly enough that it can actually last. The tricky version is holding on past the point of comfort because change feels too disruptive, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is remembering that stability can include movement, not just sameness. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Taurus part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
