5th House in Leo — Creativity, romance, play, pleasure, and the part of you that wants to express itself
In creativity, romance, and play, your style tends to be warm, expressive, and heart-led. This can be a real strength when it helps you with bringing confidence, creativity, and life into a room. The tricky version is feeling unseen too quickly or turning vulnerability into pride, especially when you are trying to feel safe or in control. What this house seems to ask from you is letting yourself shine without needing constant proof that you matter. In practice, this area flows best when you let the Leo part of you be genuine rather than exaggerated.
