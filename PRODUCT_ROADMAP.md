# AstroMYLA Product Roadmap

## Current v1.7 product position

AstroMYLA intentionally competes on clarity and interpretation quality rather than copying every calculator offered by long-established astrology portals.

Current primary tools:

- Natal / Birth Chart
- Synastry bi-wheel + Connection Profile
- Midpoint Composite
- Astrocartography angular lines
- Free PDFs
- Premium long-form natal PDFs
- iyzico hosted-checkout foundation

## Highest-value missing tools

### v1.8 — Predictive Astrology

1. Current transits
2. Natal + transit bi-wheel
3. Transit-to-natal interpretation
4. Solar Return
5. Secondary Progressions

These are higher priority than niche calculators because they create repeat usage: a natal chart is often generated once, while current transits can bring a user back regularly.

### v1.9 — Relationship Pro

1. Davison relationship chart
2. Composite method selector
3. Progressed synastry
4. Progressed composite
5. Relationship timeline / transit layer

### v2.0 — User Platform

1. Accounts
2. My Charts / saved people
3. Saved reports
4. Purchase history
5. “Today for You” dashboard
6. Reusable Me + Partner / Me + Friend chart selection

## Product principle

Do not turn AstroMYLA into a wall of 80 calculators. The target is:

> reliable calculations + a modern interface + transparent methods + interpretations that normal people can actually understand.
