# Relationship Astrology Methods — v1.6.0

## Synastry

AstroMYLA treats synastry as two separate natal charts interacting, not as a single compatibility score.

### Drawing
- Both charts share the same zodiac circle.
- One person's planets are drawn in the inner wheel and the other person's in the outer wheel.
- Inter-chart aspects are drawn between the two charts.
- The inner person's house cusps are shown.
- **Swap inner chart** reverses the perspective so each person's planets can be inspected in the other person's houses.

This follows the common bi-wheel/outer-circle convention used by major astrology chart systems.

### Interpretation
The reading prioritizes:
1. Tight cross-chart aspects.
2. Repeating themes across several contacts.
3. Emotional/Sun-Moon patterns.
4. Mercury communication contacts.
5. Venus/Mars attraction and desire.
6. Jupiter/Saturn growth, responsibility and pressure.
7. Uranus/Neptune/Pluto freedom, idealization and intensity.
8. Directional house overlays in both directions when reliable birth times are available.

If a person's birth time is unknown, time-sensitive Moon contacts, that person's angles, and house overlays requiring that chart are omitted rather than guessed.

## Composite

AstroMYLA uses the **zodiacal midpoint method**. Each composite planet is the shortest-arc midpoint between the corresponding natal positions.

When both exact birth times are known, the corresponding natal angles and house cusps are also midpointed to provide a symbolic relationship-house layer. This is labelled as symbolic; it is not represented as a literal event chart with an invented date/place.

If either time is unknown, composite houses are omitted.

Composite interpretation is kept conceptually separate from synastry:
- Synastry = two people activating each other.
- Composite = the symbolic pattern of the relationship as a unit.

## Astrocartography

The current map computes geocentric angular lines for Sun through Pluto:
- ASC: rising
- DSC: setting
- MC: culminating
- IC: lower meridian

It does not claim to calculate parans, Local Space, or a full relocated house chart. Exact birth time is required.
